# IMAGE PROMPTS — The Paradise Lodge

For gpt-image-2. St Kilda, Melbourne, February 1994.

Two modes below. **Reference sheets** are flat, neutral, and exist to model against. **In-world assets** are period-stylised and go into the game as textures.

Don't mix them. A stylised character portrait is useless as modelling reference.

---

## How to use this

1. Generate each character sheet first. Pick the best of three or four seeds.
2. Feed the winning sheet back in as a reference image for any further images of that character. Identity drifts badly without it.
3. Australian faces, not American. If a result looks like a US casting call, regenerate.

---

## MODE A — Character reference sheets

Shared preamble. Prepend to every sheet prompt:

> Character reference sheet for 3D modelling. Flat even studio lighting, no dramatic shadows, no rim light. Neutral mid-grey seamless background. Three views in a single horizontal row at consistent scale and eye level: front, three-quarter left, profile left. Full body, arms relaxed at sides, neutral expression, feet shoulder width apart. Sharp focus throughout, no depth of field, no vignette, no film grain. Photorealistic. Australian, 1994.

## MODE B — In-world assets

These go into the game as textures. Fully period-stylised.

### The photo in the frame

The most important image in the game. Generate ten and pick one.

> A 1994 amateur snapshot photograph, 35mm point and shoot camera with direct on-camera flash. Landscape, 4:3.
>
> A young blonde woman in her late twenties, petite, in a pale cream dress, sitting at a pub table beside a heavily muscled man in his early thirties with a dark ponytail and a full goatee, wearing a plain black shirt. His arm is around her shoulders. Both are laughing at something off camera, unposed, caught mid-moment. Beer glasses and an ashtray on the table in front of them. Dim Australian public bar behind them, out of focus, patterned carpet, warm tungsten light.
>
> Harsh direct flash falling off fast into darkness at the edges. Slight red-eye. Colour cast warm and slightly green. Visible film grain. Minor over-exposure on their faces. Sharp in the centre, soft at the corners. Slightly crooked framing.
>
> A small orange digital date stamp printed in the lower right corner reading: 12 3 1993
>
> This is a real photograph of real people, not a posed studio image.

The date stamp puts it eleven months before her death, roughly when they started. That's why she kept it face down.

### The note

> Extreme close-up photograph of a small square of lined notepaper, torn from a pad, lying on a dark timber dresser in warm afternoon sunlight. Handwritten in blue biro,女 female handwriting, quick and cheerful, slightly rounded, with a small underline. The text reads exactly: fly out April 6th! Can't wait!
>
> Shallow depth of field, sharp on the paper. Warm low sun raking across the surface, paper texture visible, faint indentation from the pen. 1994. Photorealistic.

*(Strip the stray character before use — write "in quick cheerful rounded female handwriting".)*

### The neon sign

> A 1990s Australian hotel neon sign mounted above the entrance of a dilapidated grand Victorian mansion, photographed at dusk. Two lines of neon tube lettering in a period script and block combination. The upper line reads: THE PARADISE LODGE. The lower line reads: Rooms to let.
>
> The upper line glows hot pink, the lower line glows cyan. One tube in the upper line is dead and unlit. Weathered mounting brackets, rust streaks down the rendered wall beneath. Overcast dusk, sign is the brightest thing in frame. Photorealistic, sharp, legible.

### The diary page

> Extreme close-up of an open page of a small personal diary, 1994, photographed flat under even light. Lined paper, handwritten in blue biro in quick rounded female handwriting. One entry is clearly legible and centred in frame, reading exactly: meeting M for lunch - mahoneys 3pm Tuesday
>
> Other entries above and below are visible but out of focus and unreadable. Paper slightly cockled, a faint coffee ring at the top corner. Photorealistic, sharp on the centre line.

## Notes

- **Say "no text" on every character sheet.** Uniforms and clothing are where models hallucinate logos.
- **The date stamp, the note and the sign are the text-critical images.** Generate those at the highest quality setting and check character by character.
- Everything in Mode B should look like it was photographed in 1994, not like a 2026 image with a filter on it. If it looks clean, regenerate.
